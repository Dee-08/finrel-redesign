# Content and design audit

Reviewed 24 September 2026 before implementation.

## Design authority

1. Original Finrel brand and supplied company brief.
2. `FINREL_DESIGN_CONSTITUTION.md`, including sections 27–55 and the appended brand-preservation rules.
3. Supplied Cénée screenshot and `Screenshot Style.md`.

The screenshot contributes composition, scale and rhythm. Its cosmetics identity, logo, copy and proprietary assets are not used. The styling excerpt contains many third-party widget defaults; those are not copied indiscriminately. Its useful principles are the 12-column grid, generous outer margins, 30px-scale gaps, strong display type, restrained colour, and approximately 300ms transitions. Finrel uses an original-logo header, its real business photography, Manrope body/display typography and a restrained Georgia italic accent. Square edges and open editorial layouts replace repeated rounded cards.

## Original website observations

- Original navigation: Welcome, About Us, Services, Contact and Shop Now.
- Original logo: `wp-content/uploads/2021/08/finrels-logo.png`; original favicon retained.
- Main orange from stylesheet: `#f05a1a`. Green tones include `#84aa2c`, `#83a846`, and the brighter lime in the supplied logo. Dark green neutrals support legible text and buttons; the logo colours are not changed.
- Existing typography is conventional Enfold/WordPress sans-serif with repeated uppercase labels. The redesign makes hierarchy and editorial composition more intentional.
- Existing homepage: auto-rotating announcements and service slides, appointment/store/about action blocks, service claims, eatery announcement, rotating branch photos and counters.
- Existing form incorrectly offers Dentistry, Neurology, Diagnostic Imaging and Pediatrics. These are not treated as Finrel services. The new form uses verified business categories, and the pharmacist request flow is branch-specific.
- The original homepage includes an incomplete relative shop link in one slide. All redesigned shop links use the full HTTPS store URL.
- The online store could not be reached through the research browser tool. The supplied link is preserved; availability and checkout remain an external dependency.

- Unclear 24/7 helpline: no 24-hour or emergency-service claim, and no invented veterinary-specific phone.
